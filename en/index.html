OUTBREAK

A text-based role-playing and survival game, played entirely in a terminal. Built to learn the Go language by going beyond simple syntax exercises: a real game loop, from character creation to boss fights.

  • 4 playable classes: Survivor, Punk, Field Medic, Saviour
  • A shelter to manage, a day/night cycle and the risk of night raids
  • Expeditions across 5 zones with waves of infected and bosses
  • Turn-based combat, resources, black market and workshop
  • Casino with a full Blackjack game and roulette
  • Custom terminal UI: 24-bit colours, gradients, ASCII logo
